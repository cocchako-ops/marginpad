MarginPad is a free, no-signup toolkit that gives crypto futures traders the six numbers they need on every trade. Calculate your exact liquidation price for any leverage (long or short), size positions by risk using the 1% rule, work out PnL and ROE, blend a dollar-cost-average entry, build a take-profit ladder, and check your risk/reward ratio and break-even win rate.
